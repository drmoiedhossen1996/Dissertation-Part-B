# Citation insertion guide — Dissertation Part B

Line-by-line guide to inserting citations with the Zotero Word plugin.
Companion file: **MASTER-CITATION-LIBRARY.md** — build the library from that first.

**How to use:** find the anchor phrase in Word, place the cursor immediately after it, insert the
paper named in the right-hand column. Work down each table in order.

---

## Before you start

1. Build the Zotero library from MASTER-CITATION-LIBRARY.md and verify all 17 items.
2. Set the citation style in Zotero (Vancouver numeric, subject to handbook confirmation).
3. **Delete the existing reference lists** from Introduction.docx, Methods.docx and Discussion.docx.
   Two errors are live in the Introduction list — Garcia in the wrong journal, and Binter 2016 carrying
   Binter 2017's author list plus a bracketed placeholder — and the Discussion has no list at all.
   Deleting all three makes that moot; Zotero generates one merged bibliography.
4. Insert **by claim**, not by the old superscript number. Four of the existing markers point at the
   wrong paper (see "Corrections" at the end).

## After insertion — five checks

1. **The four author-pair traps.** Confirm each resolves to the intended paper:
   Binter 2016 (*Magn Reson Med*) at Introduction 1.2 and Discussion 4.2, 4.4 ·
   Binter 2017 (*Circ Cardiovasc Imaging*) at Introduction 1.3, 1.4 and Discussion 4.4 ·
   Kamphuis 2018 (*Int J Cardiovasc Imaging*) at Introduction 1.2 and Discussion 4.5 ·
   Kamphuis 2021 (*Am J Physiol*) at Discussion 4.2 only.
2. **No orphans.** Every paper in the generated bibliography should be cited at least once, and all 17
   should appear.
3. **No placeholders.** Search the whole document for `[`, `TBC`, `CONFIRM`, `VERIFY`.
4. **Results has no citations.**
5. **Recount the word total** — the bibliography is normally excluded, but confirm against the handbook.
